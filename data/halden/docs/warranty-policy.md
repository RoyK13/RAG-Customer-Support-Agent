# Warranty Policy

Every new Halden appliance comes with the Halden Standard Warranty. This page explains what the warranty covers, how long it lasts, and how to file a claim.

## Coverage

### What is covered

The Halden Standard Warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use. If a covered part fails, Halden will repair or replace the part at no cost to you, including labor.

### Coverage periods

- Parts and labor: 2 years from the date of delivery.
- Sealed components (dishwasher pump motor and washer drive motor): 5 years from the date of delivery, parts only. Labor for sealed components is covered for the first 2 years.
- Stainless steel wash tub (dishwashers) and drum (washers): 10 years, parts only.

The warranty starts on the delivery date shown on your order confirmation, not the purchase date.

### Refurbished products

Products sold as Halden Certified Refurbished carry a 1 year parts and labor warranty. Sealed component and tub coverage does not apply to refurbished products.

## Exclusions

The warranty does not cover:

- Damage caused by improper installation that did not follow the Halden installation guide.
- Cosmetic damage such as scratches or dents that were not reported within 7 days of delivery.
- Commercial use, including restaurants, laundromats, and rental properties with shared laundry rooms.
- Consumable parts such as filters, door gaskets after the first year, and light bulbs.
- Damage from power surges, floods, fire, or pests.

Using the appliance in a home you rent out to a single household is not considered commercial use.

## Transferring the warranty

The Halden Standard Warranty transfers automatically to a new owner if the appliance stays at its original installation address. If the appliance is moved to a new address by a new owner, the warranty ends.

## Filing a claim

### Before you file

Check the troubleshooting guide for your product first. Many error codes can be cleared without a technician visit.

### How to file a claim

1. Sign in to your Halden account and open **Orders**.
2. Select the appliance and choose **Start a warranty claim**.
3. Describe the problem and upload a photo of the rating label, which is located inside the door frame on dishwashers and behind the detergent drawer on washers.
4. Choose a time window for a technician visit.

If you do not have an account, you can file a claim by phone at 1-800-555-0142. Have your order number and the model and serial number ready.

### What happens next

A Halden support agent reviews each claim within 2 business days. Approved claims are scheduled with an authorized technician, usually within 5 business days. If a repair is not possible, Halden replaces the appliance with the same model or a model of equal or greater value.
