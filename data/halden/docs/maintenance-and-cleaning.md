# Maintenance and Cleaning

Regular cleaning keeps Halden appliances running well and prevents many error codes.

## DW-450 dishwasher

### Cleaning the filter

Clean the filter once a month, or more often if you do not rinse dishes before loading. Twist the cylindrical filter counterclockwise, lift it out with the flat mesh filter, and rinse both under warm running water. Use a soft brush for stuck food. Do not use a wire brush.

### Cleaning the spray arms

If dishes come out dirty, check the spray arm holes for blockages. Pull the lower spray arm straight up to remove it. Clear the holes with a toothpick and rinse the arm.

### Rinse aid

Refill the rinse aid dispenser when the rinse aid indicator light turns on. A full dispenser lasts about one month.

## WM-820 washer

### Tub Clean cycle

Run the Tub Clean cycle once a month, or when the washer shows the tC reminder. Use an empty drum and either a washer cleaning tablet or 1 cup of chlorine bleach in the detergent drawer.

### Door gasket

Wipe the rubber door gasket and the glass dry after the last wash of the day. Leave the door slightly open between washes to prevent odors and mold.

### Detergent drawer

Remove the detergent drawer once a month by pressing the release tab and pulling it out. Rinse it under warm water.

### Drain pump filter

Clean the drain pump filter every 3 months. Follow the steps for error code F02 in the WM-820 troubleshooting guide.

## Replacement parts

Order genuine Halden replacement parts, filters, and cleaning tablets from the Parts section of halden.example. Non-genuine parts can void the warranty if they cause damage.
