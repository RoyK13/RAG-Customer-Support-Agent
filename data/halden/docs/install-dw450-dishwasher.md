# Installation Guide - Halden Aria DW-450 Dishwasher

The Halden Aria DW-450 is a 24 inch built-in dishwasher. Follow this guide to install it under a standard kitchen counter. Installation takes about 2 hours.

## Before you begin

### Tools you need

- Phillips and flat screwdrivers
- Adjustable wrench
- Level
- Tape measure
- Drill with a 1/8 inch bit
- Bucket and towels

### Parts in the box

- Dishwasher with drain hose attached
- Mounting bracket kit (2 top brackets, 2 side brackets, 4 screws)
- Water inlet elbow (3/4 inch hose thread to 3/8 inch compression)
- Toe kick panel
- Hose clamp

### Site requirements

- Cabinet opening: 24 inches wide, 34 to 35 inches high, and at least 23 inches deep.
- Power: a dedicated 120 V, 60 Hz, 15 A circuit. The DW-450 ships with a power cord and plugs into a grounded outlet in the cabinet next to the opening.
- Water supply: hot water line with a shutoff valve, water pressure between 20 and 120 psi, water temperature of 120 F at the dishwasher.
- Drain: a garbage disposer inlet or a sink drain tailpiece with a branch connection.

## Installation steps

### Step 1: Prepare the opening

Turn off the hot water at the shutoff valve. Check that the floor in the opening is level. Drill a 1 1/2 inch hole in the side of the sink cabinet for the drain hose, water line, and power cord.

### Step 2: Connect the water inlet

Wrap the threads of the water inlet elbow with plumber's tape and screw it into the inlet valve at the front left corner of the dishwasher. Connect the hot water line to the elbow and tighten with a wrench. Do not overtighten.

### Step 3: Route the drain hose

Feed the drain hose through the hole into the sink cabinet. Loop the hose up to the underside of the counter to create a high loop at least 20 inches above the floor. The high loop prevents dirty sink water from flowing back into the dishwasher. Connect the hose to the disposer inlet or drain tailpiece and secure it with the hose clamp.

If you connect to a garbage disposer, remove the disposer's knockout plug before connecting the hose. The dishwasher will not drain if the plug is still in place.

### Step 4: Slide the dishwasher into place

Slide the dishwasher into the opening while pulling the hose and cord through the cabinet hole. Adjust the four leveling legs until the dishwasher is level front to back and side to side. The top of the door should be about 1/4 inch below the counter.

### Step 5: Secure the dishwasher

Use the top brackets for laminate or wood counters. Use the side brackets for stone counters, which cannot be drilled. Screw the brackets into the counter or cabinet sides.

### Step 6: Test the installation

Turn on the water and plug in the dishwasher. Run a Rinse Only cycle with the toe kick panel removed and check all connections for leaks. Attach the toe kick panel when there are no leaks.

## After installation

Register your dishwasher in your Halden account to make warranty claims faster. Keep this guide, because improper installation is not covered by the warranty.
