# Return Policy

This page explains how to return a Halden appliance or accessory, what qualifies for a return, and how refunds work.

## Return window

### Appliances

You can return an appliance within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The appliance must be uninstalled, clean, and in its original condition.

### Accessories and parts

Unopened accessories and replacement parts can be returned within 60 days of delivery. Opened filters and detergent products cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

### Holiday purchases

Appliances delivered between November 15 and December 31 can be returned until January 31 of the following year.

## Restocking fees

- Unopened appliance: no restocking fee.
- Installed or used appliance returned within 30 days: 15% restocking fee.
- Appliance returned because of a defect or shipping damage: no restocking fee.

Halden Certified Refurbished appliances can be returned within 14 days, with a 10% restocking fee if used.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in to your Halden account and open **Orders**.
2. Choose **Return an item** and select a reason.
3. Halden schedules a pickup. A driver collects large appliances from your home. You do not need to repackage them in the original box, but the appliance must be disconnected from water and power before the pickup.

Accessories can be returned by mail using the prepaid label emailed to you after you start the return.

### Return pickup fee

Return pickup is free for defective or damaged items. For all other appliance returns, Halden charges a $49 pickup fee, which is deducted from the refund.

## Refunds

### Refund timing

Halden issues the refund within 5 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection. Your bank may take another 3 to 7 business days to show the credit.

### Refund method

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Orders paid with Halden Pay financing are refunded to the financing account, and the remaining balance is adjusted.

## Exchanges

Halden does not process direct exchanges. To get a different model, return the original item and place a new order.
