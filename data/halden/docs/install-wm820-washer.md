# Installation Guide - Halden Nordic WM-820 Front-Load Washer

The Halden Nordic WM-820 is a 27 inch front-load washer with a 4.5 cubic foot drum. This guide covers unpacking, connecting, and leveling the washer. Installation takes about 1 hour.

## Before you begin

### Tools you need

- Adjustable wrench
- Pliers
- Level
- Bucket

### Parts in the box

- Washer
- Two inlet hoses (hot and cold)
- Drain hose guide
- Four transit bolt caps
- Spanner for transit bolts

### Site requirements

- Floor: a solid, level floor. The slope under the washer must be less than 1 inch from front to back or side to side.
- Space: at least 1 inch on each side and 4 inches behind the washer for hoses.
- Power: a grounded 120 V, 60 Hz, 15 A outlet within 4 feet of the washer.
- Water supply: hot and cold faucets with water pressure between 20 and 116 psi.
- Drain: a standpipe between 24 and 96 inches tall, or a laundry tub.

## Installation steps

### Step 1: Remove the transit bolts

The WM-820 ships with four transit bolts on the back panel that lock the drum in place during shipping. Loosen each bolt with the supplied spanner and pull it out. Cover the holes with the transit bolt caps.

Keep the transit bolts. You need them if you move the washer to a new home. Running the washer with the transit bolts installed causes severe vibration and can damage the washer, and this damage is not covered by the warranty.

### Step 2: Connect the inlet hoses

Attach the hose with the red end to the hot inlet and the blue end to the cold inlet on the back of the washer. Connect the other ends to the matching faucets. Tighten by hand, then a further two thirds of a turn with pliers. Turn on the faucets and check for leaks.

### Step 3: Position the drain hose

Hook the drain hose into the standpipe or laundry tub using the drain hose guide. Do not push the hose more than 6 inches into the standpipe. Do not seal the hose into the standpipe, because the washer needs an air gap to drain properly.

### Step 4: Level the washer

Place a level on top of the washer. Adjust the four leveling feet by turning them until the washer is level and all four feet touch the floor firmly. Tighten the lock nuts on each foot against the washer body.

### Step 5: Test the washer

Plug in the washer. Run a Rinse and Spin cycle without laundry. Check the hoses for leaks and watch for excessive movement during the spin.

## Stacking with a dryer

The WM-820 can be stacked with the Halden Nordic DR-820 dryer using the Halden stacking kit (part number SK-27), sold separately.
