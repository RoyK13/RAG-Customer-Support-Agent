# Billing FAQ

Answers to common questions about charges, receipts, and sales tax on Halden orders.

## Charges

### When will I be charged?

Halden places a temporary authorization hold on your card when you place the order. Your card is charged when the order ships. Accessories that ship separately are charged when each package ships.

### Why do I see two charges?

If your order has items that ship separately, you see one charge per shipment. You may also see an authorization hold that has not been released yet. Holds usually disappear within 5 business days.

### Can I get a price adjustment?

If the price of an item you bought drops within 14 days of delivery, Halden refunds the difference once per item. Request a price adjustment by contacting support with your order number. Price adjustments do not apply to clearance, refurbished, or flash sale items.

## Receipts and invoices

### Where can I find my receipt?

Open **Orders** in your Halden account and choose **View invoice**. You can download the invoice as a PDF.

### Can I change the name on an invoice?

Yes. Contact support within 30 days of the order date to reissue an invoice with a different name or a business name.

## Sales tax

### Do you charge sales tax?

Halden collects sales tax in every state where it delivers, based on the delivery address. Sales tax is shown at checkout before you pay.

### Are recycling fees taxed?

Some states charge an appliance recycling fee. The fee appears as a separate line on your invoice. Whether it is taxed depends on your state.

## Payment problems

### My payment was declined

Check that the billing address matches the address on file with your card issuer. If the payment is still declined, try another payment method or contact your card issuer. Halden support cannot see why a bank declined a payment.
