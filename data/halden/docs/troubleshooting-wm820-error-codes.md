# Troubleshooting - WM-820 Washer Error Codes

The Halden Nordic WM-820 displays an error code when it stops a cycle because of a problem. Find your code below. If the code returns after these steps, file a warranty claim.

## How to reset the washer

Press **Power** to turn the washer off, unplug it for 5 minutes, then plug it back in and turn it on.

## Water codes

### F01: Water inlet problem

The washer did not fill within 8 minutes.

1. Check that both faucets are fully open.
2. Turn off the faucets, disconnect the inlet hoses, and clean the mesh screens inside the washer inlets with a small brush.
3. Check the hoses for kinks.

### F02: Drain problem

The washer did not drain within 6 minutes.

1. Open the small door at the bottom right of the front panel.
2. Place a shallow pan under the emergency drain hose, pull it out, and remove the cap to drain the remaining water.
3. Turn the drain pump filter counterclockwise and remove it. Clear lint, coins, or other objects.
4. Replace the filter and close the door.
5. Check that the drain hose is not pushed more than 6 inches into the standpipe.

### F05: Overflow

The water level is higher than normal. The washer drains automatically when this code appears. Turn off the faucets and contact Halden support if the code returns.

## Door and lock codes

### dL: Door lock failure

The door did not lock at the start of the cycle. Make sure no clothing is caught in the door, close it firmly, and restart. If dL continues, the door lock may need replacement.

### dO: Door open

The door was opened during a cycle or is not closed. Close the door and press **Start**.

## Load and motor codes

### UB: Unbalanced load

The washer could not balance the load for the spin. This happens most often with a single heavy item such as a bath mat or a small load of towels. Redistribute the laundry or add a few more items, then press **Start**.

If UB appears with normal loads, check that the washer is level and that the transit bolts were removed during installation.

### E21: Motor fault

The drive motor did not respond. Reset the washer. If E21 appears again, do not use the washer and contact Halden support. The drive motor is a sealed component covered for 5 years under the Standard Warranty.

## Maintenance reminder

### tC: Tub clean reminder

tC is not an error. The washer shows tC every 40 cycles to remind you to run the Tub Clean cycle. The reminder clears after the Tub Clean cycle finishes.
