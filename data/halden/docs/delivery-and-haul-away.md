# Delivery Services and Haul Away

Halden offers optional services when your appliance is delivered. Add them at checkout or from the **Orders** page up to 48 hours before your delivery window.

## Room of choice delivery

Standard delivery leaves the appliance at the first dry area inside your front door or garage. Room of choice delivery places it in the room where it will be installed.

Room of choice delivery costs $29. Drivers can carry appliances up or down one flight of stairs. Each additional flight costs $15.

## Professional installation

Halden installation partners connect the appliance to existing water, drain, and power connections.

- Dishwasher installation: $149
- Washer installation: $99

Installation does not include new plumbing, electrical work, cabinet modifications, or removing countertops. If the installer finds that your connections do not meet the requirements in the installation guide, they will not install the appliance, and the installation fee is refunded.

## Haul away of old appliances

Drivers can remove your old appliance on the day of delivery for $25 per appliance. The old appliance must be disconnected from water and power and be easy to reach. Halden recycles hauled away appliances through certified recyclers.

Haul away is only available on the same visit as a new appliance delivery.

## Inspecting your delivery

Inspect the appliance with the driver before signing. Note any visible damage on the delivery receipt. Report hidden damage within 7 days of delivery through **Orders** > **Report a problem**.
