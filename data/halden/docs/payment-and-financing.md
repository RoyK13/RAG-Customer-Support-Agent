# Payment Methods and Halden Pay Financing

This page lists the payment methods Halden accepts and explains Halden Pay monthly financing.

## Accepted payment methods

Halden accepts the following payment methods for online orders:

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit and debit cards
- PayPal
- Halden gift cards
- Halden Pay financing

You can combine up to two Halden gift cards with one other payment method on a single order. Halden does not accept cash, checks, or wire transfers for online orders.

## Halden Pay financing

### How Halden Pay works

Halden Pay lets you split the cost of an order into equal monthly payments. Halden Pay is available on orders between $300 and $5,000. Approval is subject to a credit check by Halden's financing partner, which does not affect your credit score until you accept a plan.

### Plan terms

| Plan | Interest rate | Minimum order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 months | 0% APR | $300 |
| 12 months | 0% APR | $800 |
| 24 months | 9.99% APR | $1,200 |

There are no fees for paying off a Halden Pay plan early.

### Missed payments

If a monthly payment is more than 10 days late, a $15 late fee applies. Two consecutive missed payments on a 0% APR plan convert the remaining balance to a 24.99% APR.

### Managing your plan

Sign in to your Halden account and open **Payments** > **Halden Pay** to see your balance, change your payment date, or make an extra payment. You can change your payment date once every 6 months.

## Gift cards

Halden gift cards never expire and have no fees. Check a gift card balance under **Payments** > **Gift cards** in your account. Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash, except where required by law.
