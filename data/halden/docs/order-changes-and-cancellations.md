# Order Changes and Cancellations

How to change or cancel a Halden order before it is delivered.

## Changing an order

### Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address until the order ships. Open **Orders**, choose the order, and select **Change address**. After the order ships, contact support. Address changes after shipping are not always possible and may move your delivery window.

### Adding or removing items

Items cannot be added to an order after it is placed. Place a new order for additional items. You can remove an item by cancelling it, as long as that item has not shipped.

### Changing the color or model

To change the color or model of an appliance, cancel the item and place a new order.

## Cancelling an order

### Before shipping

You can cancel any item at no charge before it ships. Open **Orders**, choose the order, and select **Cancel items**. The authorization hold on your card is released within 5 business days.

### After shipping

Once a large appliance has shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery when the driver arrives, and Halden processes it as a return with no pickup fee. Alternatively, accept the delivery and start a return within 30 days.

### Backordered items

If an item is backordered, Halden emails you the expected ship date. You can cancel a backordered item at any time before it ships.

## Price changes after ordering

If the price drops before your order ships, contact support and Halden updates the order to the lower price.
