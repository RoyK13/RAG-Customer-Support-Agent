# Account Help

How to manage your Halden account, sign in, and keep your account secure.

## Signing in

### Resetting your password

1. On the sign in page, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Open the reset link in the email within 60 minutes. The link expires after that.
4. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters.

If you do not receive the email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder.

### Locked accounts

After 5 failed sign in attempts, your account is locked for 30 minutes. Wait 30 minutes or reset your password to unlock it right away.

## Account security

### Two-step verification

Turn on two-step verification under **Account** > **Security**. Halden supports authenticator apps and text message codes. Halden recommends authenticator apps.

### Signing out of all devices

If you think someone else has access to your account, choose **Sign out everywhere** under **Account** > **Security**, then change your password.

## Updating your details

### Changing your email address

Go to **Account** > **Profile** and choose **Change email**. Halden sends a confirmation link to the new address. The change takes effect after you open the link.

### Saved addresses

You can save up to 10 delivery addresses under **Account** > **Addresses**.

### Communication preferences

Choose which marketing emails and texts you receive under **Account** > **Notifications**. Order and delivery messages cannot be turned off.

## Product registration

Register appliances under **Account** > **My appliances** by entering the model and serial number. Appliances bought from halden.example are registered automatically. Registering an appliance bought from another retailer makes warranty claims faster, but you need your proof of purchase to file a claim.

## Closing your account

To close your account, contact Halden support. Accounts with an open Halden Pay balance or an active Care Plus plan cannot be closed until the balance is paid or the plan ends or is cancelled. Halden deletes account data within 30 days of closure, except order records that must be kept for tax purposes.
