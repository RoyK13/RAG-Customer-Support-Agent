# Shipping and Delivery Times

Halden delivers to the 48 contiguous US states. This page lists delivery times and shipping costs.

## Delivery areas

### Where we deliver

Halden delivers to the 48 contiguous US states. Halden does not currently deliver to Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, PO boxes, or addresses outside the United States.

### Rural addresses

Some rural ZIP codes have a longer delivery window. The checkout page shows the expected window for your ZIP code before you pay.

## Delivery times

### Large appliances

| Delivery type | Typical time |
| --- | --- |
| Standard delivery | 5 to 8 business days |
| Express delivery | 2 to 3 business days |
| Rural standard delivery | 8 to 12 business days |

Express delivery is not available for rural ZIP codes.

### Accessories and parts

Accessories and replacement parts ship separately by parcel carrier and usually arrive in 3 to 5 business days.

## Shipping costs

- Standard delivery for large appliances: free on orders over $499, otherwise $59.
- Express delivery: $99 per order.
- Accessories and parts: free on orders over $35, otherwise $6.95.

## Scheduling your delivery

After your order ships, Halden texts and emails you a link to choose a 4 hour delivery window. You can reschedule up to 24 hours before the window starts at no charge.

Someone aged 18 or older must be home to receive large appliance deliveries. If nobody is home, the driver leaves a notice and you can reschedule once for free. A second missed delivery incurs a $40 redelivery fee.

## Tracking an order

Open **Orders** in your Halden account to see the current status and tracking number. Large appliance tracking shows the delivery window on the day of delivery.
