# Contacting Halden Support

How to reach Halden customer support and what to have ready.

## Support channels

### Phone

Call 1-800-555-0142. Phone support is available Monday to Friday from 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern Time, and Saturday from 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern Time. Phone support is closed on Sundays and on US federal holidays.

### Live chat

Live chat is available in the Halden app and on halden.example during phone support hours. Chat is usually the fastest way to reach an agent.

### Email

Send email to support@halden.example. Halden replies to email within 1 business day.

## What to have ready

- Your order number, which starts with HD followed by 8 digits.
- The model number and serial number from the rating label.
- The error code shown on the display, if any.
- Photos of any damage.

## Accessibility

Customers who are deaf or hard of hearing can contact Halden through the relay service of their choice by calling 711 and asking for 1-800-555-0142.

## Feedback and complaints

If you are not satisfied with how a support request was handled, ask the agent to escalate it to a supervisor. Supervisors respond within 2 business days.
