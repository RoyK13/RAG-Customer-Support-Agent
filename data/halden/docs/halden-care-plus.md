# Halden Care Plus Extended Protection

Halden Care Plus extends protection beyond the Halden Standard Warranty. It is optional and sold separately.

## Plan options

### Plan lengths and prices

| Plan | Total coverage | Price per appliance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Care Plus 3 | 3 years from delivery | $89 |
| Care Plus 5 | 5 years from delivery | $149 |

The total coverage period includes the 2 years of the Standard Warranty. For example, Care Plus 5 adds 3 extra years of parts and labor coverage.

### What Care Plus adds

- Parts and labor for all covered parts for the full plan length, including sealed components.
- One free preventive maintenance visit per year after the Standard Warranty ends.
- Coverage for damage from power surges.
- Priority scheduling, with technician visits usually within 3 business days.

Care Plus does not cover cosmetic damage, commercial use, or damage from floods, fire, or pests.

## Buying a plan

You can add Care Plus at checkout or at any time within 60 days of delivery. After 60 days, Care Plus can no longer be purchased for that appliance.

To add a plan after checkout, open **Orders** in your Halden account and choose **Add Care Plus** next to the appliance.

## Cancelling a plan

### Full refund window

You can cancel Care Plus within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, as long as no claim has been made.

### After 30 days

After 30 days, Halden refunds the unused portion of the plan, calculated by month, minus any claims paid. Cancel from the **Orders** page or by phone.

## Claims under Care Plus

File Care Plus claims the same way as Standard Warranty claims. The claim form detects the plan automatically. There is no deductible.
